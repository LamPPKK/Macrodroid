# TFTMAC Historical Source-Build Plan — RETIRED

**Status:** SUPERSEDED — DO NOT EXECUTE
**Superseded by:** `TFTMAC_CLEAN_OWNERSHIP_PLAN.md` and `TFTMACAPP.md`

The former GPU-runtime implementation plan required a source-built Android Emulator/AEMU laboratory. That path was experimentally exercised, consumed substantial external storage, and was later superseded by the proven released Google Android Emulator runtime.

Current execution authority is:

1. Complete `TFTMAC_CLEAN_OWNERSHIP_PLAN.md` through project/storage convergence.
2. Continue the native application from `TFTMACAPP.md`.
3. Preserve the released stock Android Emulator as the control/runtime authority.
4. Do not initialize, synchronize, build, or recreate a source-built AEMU checkout during normal TFTMAC work.

Historical technical conclusions from the retired work remain represented in compact SSOT/evidence records where they still protect a current decision. The large source/build laboratory itself is disposable after the approved independence, measurement, and quiescence gates pass.

A future source-runtime effort would require a new, separately approved measured blocker proving that the released emulator cannot satisfy a required product function. The existence of old source locks or historical experiments is not sufficient authorization.
